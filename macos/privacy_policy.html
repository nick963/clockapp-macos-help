Privacy Policy for JSON Clock Viewer

Last updated: October 2025

Overview

JSON Clock Viewer respects your privacy. This app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data.

Data Collection

  • The app does not collect any personal information.
  • The app does not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • The app does not connect to external servers or cloud services for the purpose of collecting data.

All data used by the app (for example, custom clock styles you load from local JSON files) remains entirely on your device.

Third-Party Services

JSON Clock Viewer does not include or rely on any third-party SDKs or services that collect data.

Internet Access

The app does not require an internet connection for normal operation. If you choose to open external links (for example, a link to the developer’s Patreon or website), that interaction is handled by your web browser and is outside the app’s control.
